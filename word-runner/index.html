About the Game · परिचय

Learning English, Without Feeling Like Studying पढ़ाई जैसा महसूस हुए बिना अंग्रेज़ी सीखना

Most vocabulary apps feel like flashcards with extra steps. Word Runner flips that around: the running, jumping, and dodging come first, and the words are simply what you're chasing. By the time a player realizes they've learned ten new English words, they've already had fun doing it.

Built for beginners in India and anywhere else English is a second language, every piece of the interface — instructions, rewards, dialogue, and popups — speaks both Hindi and English, so no learner is ever left guessing what a button means.
